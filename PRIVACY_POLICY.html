FreedomWave — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

FreedomWave is an administration client for self-hosted Remnawave proxy/VPN panels. You connect the app to a server that you own and control by entering its URL and an API key. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Who controls your data

FreedomWave has no backend servers operated by the developer. The app does not register accounts, and it does not send your data to the developer or to any analytics, advertising, or tracking service. All administrative data you view (users, nodes, hosts, traffic, etc.) comes directly from your own Remnawave server and is governed by that server's own policies.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Server URL of your Remnawave panel.
  • API key, kept encrypted at rest using the Android Keystore (hardware-backed where available) / iOS Keychain.
  • App preferences — theme, language, biometric-lock and geo-lookup toggles.

None of this data leaves your device except the API key and requests that are sent solely to the Remnawave server you configured, over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Biometric authentication

If you enable biometric lock, authentication is performed entirely by your device's operating system (Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint). FreedomWave never receives, stores, or transmits your biometric data.

Optional IP geo-lookup

FreedomWave includes an optional "IP geo-lookup" feature (off by switch in Settings). When enabled, IP addresses taken from your panel's subscription history are sent to the third-party service ipwho.is to resolve the approximate country, region, city, and ISP for display. No other data is shared with this service. If you keep the feature disabled, no data is sent to any third party. See ipwho.is for their handling of requests.

Permissions

The app declares only standard, non-sensitive permissions:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to communicate with your Remnawave server.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT — for the optional app lock.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to refresh the home-screen widget.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, or any other sensitive runtime permission. On Android 16 the system may show a "Nearby devices" / local-network entry by default; the app neither requests nor uses it to discover or connect to nearby devices.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally and the developer holds none of it, you are in full control:

  • Disconnect / sign out inside the app to remove the stored server URL and encrypted API key.
  • Uninstall the app to permanently erase all locally stored data.

Children

FreedomWave is a technical administration tool intended for server operators and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
